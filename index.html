Our Craft

A studio where every stitch is personal

AUREL began with one sewing table and a simple belief: clothes should be made around the person who wears them. We still work that way. You are measured by hand, you choose the fabric, and the same tailor follows your garment from pattern to final press.

  • Hand-taken measurements, no guesswork
  • Premium threads and lining as standard
  • Free adjustment after your first wear
