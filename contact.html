Before You Visit

Helpful details before your appointment

A few quick notes to make your first fitting smoother and more productive.

What to bring

Carry a reference image, fabric swatch, jewellery ideas and the footwear you plan to wear so the fitting feels more accurate.

How long does it take?

Most measurements and discussions last 45 to 60 minutes. For bridal and custom work, we may recommend a second fitting.

How we confirm

After your request, we confirm the appointment by phone and share any material or sizing guidance before your visit.

Need quick advice first?

Send a note to hello@aurel.example and we will reply with the next available suggestion.
