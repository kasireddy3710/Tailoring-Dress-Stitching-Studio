The Bridal Journey

Five steps from first conversation to your big day

Bridal work takes more care than everyday tailoring. Each stage is planned with you, so the finish is ready before the day.

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We talk through the occasion, the colours you love and the mood you want the garment to carry.

  2. 02

    Design & Fabric

    Choose the silhouette, silk or brocade, lace, lining and trims. Swatches can be requested in advance.

  3. 03

    First Trial

    The first fitting checks balance, length and how the garment moves when you sit, reach and turn.

  4. 04

    Hand Finishing

    Zari, bead and thread work is added slowly, one motif at a time, by the tailor who cut the piece.

  5. 05

    Final Fitting & Delivery

    A last trial, a careful press and a garment packed for the day, with a care guide for the finish.
