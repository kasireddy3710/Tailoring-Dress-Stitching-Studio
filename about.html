Our Philosophy

Good tailoring is quiet, precise and personal

AUREL started in 2011 with one sewing table and one rule: a garment should be cut around the person who will wear it. That rule still shapes everything we do. Your measurements are taken by hand, your fabric is chosen with you, and the same tailor follows your piece from first pattern to final press.

We stitch blouses, suits, kids wear and alterations, and we give each one the same patience.

2011

One table, one promise

Started in a small room with a single machine and our first loyal clients.

2016

A proper atelier

Moved into a larger workshop with a dedicated cutting table and fitting room.

2019

The occasion line
