Studio Notes

Four habits that make a better fit

  1. 01

    Measure in your undergarments

    A padded bra or a shaping layer changes the bust and waist readings. Wear what you plan to wear with the garment.

  2. 02

    Bring the shoes you will wear

    Hem length is set with your shoes on. A hem measured on flat floor can sit too long with heels.

  3. 03

    Choose the lining with the fabric

    A lining decides how a silk or brocade garment hangs and how long it lasts. Decide both together.

  4. 04

    Ask for a second trial

    A small change at trial saves a full re-stitch later. Book the extra fitting before the final press.

Glossary

Terms we use at the cutting table

Short definitions for the words that come up in fittings, fabric choices and care. If a term is unfamiliar, ask us at your measurement session.

01

Ease

The extra room built into a garment so it can move. Too little and it pulls at the seams; too much and it looks loose.

02

Canvas

The stiff interlining that gives a jacket its chest and lapel shape. Half and full canvas differ in how much of it is hand-padded.

03

Dupion

A slubbed silk with a crisp body and a textured face. It holds structure well, which suits princess cuts.

04

Zari

Metallic thread used in festive embroidery. Keep it dry, away from perfume, and out of long exposure to sunlight.

05

Growth Allowance

Extra length and width in seams and hems, so a child's garment can be let out or let down as they grow.

06

Trial Toile

A first-fit garment, usually in a plain cloth, cut to test the balance before the real fabric is touched.
