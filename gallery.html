Fabric Guide

Choose cloth by occasion, season and wear

Fabric decides how a garment moves, breathes and ages. Start with how you will wear it, then choose the weave that suits that life.

Everyday Wear

Cotton and fine linen breathe well in warm weather and wash easily for daily blouses and shirts.

Occasions

Silk, brocade and velvet give drape and sheen for weddings, festivals and evening functions.

Office & Suits

Wool suiting and crepe hold a crisp line through long days, with canvas support for structure.

Children

Soft cottons and skin-friendly weaves, breathable linings for comfortable all-day wear.
