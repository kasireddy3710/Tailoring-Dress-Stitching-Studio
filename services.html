Fitting Guide

Know your measurements before you visit

Select a point on the figure to see how it is taken. Our tailors check every measurement with you in person, so you never have to get it perfect at home.

Point 01 of 05

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of the bust with the tape level and snug, not compressed. Stand relaxed with your arms at your sides.

Questions

Good to know before you book

How do I book a measurement appointment?

Use the Book Measurement button or call the studio during opening hours. We will confirm a slot, and you can bring any design photos or fabric swatches you have in mind.

Can I bring my own fabric?

Yes. Bring the full length you plan to use, with a little extra. Our tailor will check its weight, stretch and washing needs before cutting.

How long does a custom garment take?

Timing depends on the garment and the finish you choose. We confirm your delivery date at the measurement appointment so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you alter garments stitched elsewhere?

Yes. Bring the garment in and we will assess the fabric and seams before quoting, so we can tell you what is possible.

Is there an adjustment after delivery?

Yes. Standard packages include one free adjustment after your first wear. Fit changes after that are quoted separately.
